People

Nirav Pravinbhai Bhatt

Head of lab; Assistant Professor

Nirav Bhatt has a BE in Chemical Engineering from The M. S. University of Baroda, an MTech from IIT Madras, and a DSc from EPFL, Switzerland. He is an Assistant Professor at the Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI (WSAI) and the Department of Biotechnology, IIT Madras, and Program Chair of the MTech in Data Science and AI at the IIT Madras Zanzibar campus. He is affiliated with RBCDSAI and IBSE at IIT Madras. His research covers modelling and identification of biological and chemical systems, bioprocess control, and machine learning for engineering systems.

Roshan Balaji

PhD scholar

Roshan Balaji is a PhD scholar at IIT Madras and a PMRF fellow. He has a BTech in Biotechnology from NIT Calicut. His work covers multimodal AI and deep learning for biological systems, including enzyme benchmarks and molecular property prediction.

Adhil Ahmed

Research scholar

Adhil Ahmed is a research scholar at the Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI, IIT Madras, supported by WSAI. He works on Leak-CURBER, a leakage-controlled benchmark for enzymatic reaction tasks.

Pavan Kumar S

PhD scholar

Pavan Kumar S is a PhD scholar working with Nirav Bhatt on integrated models of biological networks. His project links metabolic, signalling, and gene regulatory networks using omics and literature data.

Rajeeva Lokshanan

PhD scholar

Rajeeva Lokshanan Reguna Madhan works with Nirav Bhatt and Himanshu Sinha. He builds machine learning and statistical models of the relationship between genotype and phenotype.

Krithika Padmanabhan

PhD scholar

Krithika Padmanabhan is a PhD scholar guided jointly by Sridharakumar Narasimhan and Nirav Bhatt. Her area is sensors, instrumentation, and control. She works on near-infrared spectroscopic analysis.

Keerthana C

PhD scholar

Keerthana C is a biotechnologist who works on bioprocess control. She uses near-infrared spectroscopy and model-based predictive control to manage the extracellular and intracellular environment of microorganisms.
