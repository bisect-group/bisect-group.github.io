Leak-CURBER

Leak-CURBER is a benchmark for enzymatic reaction tasks with leakage-aware data splits. It covers kinetic parameter prediction, binding affinity, enzyme classification, enzyme retrieval, and reaction outcome.

Metabolic modelling · bioRxiv 2026

SPECTRA

SPECTRA reconstructs metabolic models from multi-omics data at many scales. These range from minimal reactomes and context-specific models to microbial community and multi-tissue models.

Metabolic modelling · iScience 2025

ThermOptCOBRA

ThermOptCOBRA finds and removes thermodynamically infeasible cycles in genome-scale metabolic models. This gives more reliable phenotype predictions and context-specific models.

Molecule generation · arXiv 2025

NovoMolGen

NovoMolGen is a family of transformer foundation models pretrained on 1.5 billion molecules for de novo molecule generation. It outperforms prior molecular language models in unconstrained and goal-directed generation. The paper is on arXiv under the title "A Large Scale Investigation of Scaling Limits in Chemical Language Models".

Biomedical retrieval · AAAI 2026

BiCA

BiCA trains biomedical dense retrieval models with hard negatives taken from the PubMed citation graph. The repository holds the pipeline to build the corpus, train the model, and evaluate it.

Cheminformatics · JCIM 2026

Functional groups for molecular property prediction

FGMolProp predicts molecular properties from the functional groups in a molecule. This gives representations that chemists can interpret, and it works on more than 35 datasets.
