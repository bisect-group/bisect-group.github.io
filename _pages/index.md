The BiSECt lab (BioSystems Engineering and Control) is led by Dr. Nirav Pravinbhai Bhatt at the Wadhwani School of Data Science and AI, IIT Madras. We build models of biological and chemical systems from data. Our work covers metabolic network reconstruction, reaction network identification, machine learning benchmarks for enzymes and molecules, and process control. We release our methods as open-source software.
